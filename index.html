Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Applies to Sniper Syndicate: Roach City on Android (com.poyraz.snipersyndicate), iOS and desktop.

Who we are

Sniper Syndicate: Roach City ("the Game") is developed and published by Poyraz Yazılım (Ömer Poyraz), Türkiye. Contact: omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com.

Summary

The Game is single-player and works offline. There is no account and no analytics or tracking SDK. Two optional online extras use a small server of ours (section 1a). We do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location. The only third-party services built in are Google AdMob (ads, mobile only), Google Play Billing (purchases on Android) and Apple's App Store (on iOS).

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (missions, stars, cash, rifles, upgrades, medals, daily contracts, story choices, purchase status) and settings (volume, graphics, language, controls) are saved only in the Game's private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

If you tap "Save kill photo", the Game saves one image of your in-game shot to your device's Pictures folder. It is never uploaded; sharing it is up to you.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob (Android and iOS)

The free version shows interstitial ads only between missions (never during gameplay, and not in your first missions) and optional rewarded videos only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the Game shows Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the Game: Settings → Ad choices. On iOS the system asks whether the Game may track you; if you refuse, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.

"Remove Ads" owners see no interstitial ads. The desktop version contains no ads.

3. In-app purchases

Optional one-time purchases (Remove Ads, Double Cash, Rifle Arsenal) are processed entirely by Google Play or Apple. We never see your payment details. The Game only receives the list of products you own, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new install. Nothing sold is required to finish the Game.

4. Children

The Game contains stylised gun violence and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Permissions

Android: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and Google Play), VIBRATE (shot feedback, can be turned off in Settings), and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (declared by the AdMob SDK).

6. Deletion and contact

All game data lives on your device: clear the app's data or uninstall to delete it. For data processed by Google use your Google Account controls; for Apple, your Apple ID settings. Questions: omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
